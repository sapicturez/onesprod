Photographer

Tomek Albin

Tomek Albin is a visual artist, who successfully combines distinctive conceptual work as a photographer and as a video director. Both in his art and commercial projects, he captures pristine, and perfectly planned landscapes. Work with long exposures, cinematic light and detailed compositions characterises also his sterile still life photographs, moody automotive projects as well as creative commercial videos, inspired by geometry and sculpture. His distinctive style has resulted in multiple collaborations with major advertising agencies, clients and galleries from across the globe.

Consistently, he’s well-respected as a photography lecturer at the National Film School in Lodz where he leads conceptual workshop and conducts his PhD.

Some of his clients include: Toyota, Nike, Reebok, Unilever, Skoda, Renault, Dacia, Belvedere Vodka, LG, Avon, Volvo, Procter & Gamble, Lot-Polish Airlines, Nestle, Tchibo, Netia, Mercedes Benz, Citi Bank, WWF, Orange, Pizza Hut, Discovery, Lays, Orlen
